[ 05 // SYSTEM MISSION ]

Bridging every student to campus life.

At Christ University Pune Lavasa ("The Hub of Analytics"), campus life is rich with student initiatives. However, students frequently miss club inductions due to fragmented channels and feel left out.

ClubCentral provides a single, high-fidelity source of truth. We celebrate the unique identity of all 23 clubs, provide transparent breakdowns of activities, and connect students directly to coordinators.

How Joining Works

3-Step Campus Integration Pipeline:

01

Inspect Node & Enquire

Browse clubs or launch the Matcher, click "Enquire More" on any club to review full objectives and coordinators.

02

Submit Expression of Interest

Provide your student register number, program, and statement through the creative club join action.

03

Induction & Onboarding

Student heads and faculty coordinators review expressions of interest, verify via university email, and induct you into active sessions.
